## Design Context

### Users

The primary users are Project Controls Engineers and Project Controls Managers working on EPC projects from laptop and desktop environments. Their core job is to upload governed monthly project-control workbooks, confirm data readiness, run deterministic checks, triage cost, schedule, progress, and data-quality findings, inspect traceable evidence, update finding status, and export useful results with confidence. The free public beta must support real project-control work while giving first-time visitors a safe, instant synthetic-data demo. Verified users receive isolated personal trial workspaces; team administration and paid-plan management remain deferred.

### Brand Personality

ControlCheck AI should feel precise, intelligent, modern, and industrial. The emotional goal is calm confidence: serious risks must be unmistakable, while routine analysis should feel controlled rather than alarming. The brand promise is expressed by the approved tagline: **Check. Control. Confidence.**

### Aesthetic Direction

Use an **Industrial Intelligence** direction: a modern engineering control room translated into a refined light-mode interface. Use the approved ControlCheck AI logo and its deep navy, cobalt, and cyan identity. Reserve gradients for the logo and rare high-value moments; avoid neon sci-fi dashboards, decorative glow, generic AI imagery, excessive cards, and visual noise. Use warm, lightly tinted surfaces and engineering-drawing structure to support dense operational data. Keep semantic risk colors independent from the brand palette: amber and red indicate risk, while green or teal indicates healthy states. Use the full logo on access and onboarding surfaces, and the compact C/check mark for navigation, favicon, and constrained layouts. Prepare light-background, dark-background, and icon-only logo variants.

### Design Principles

1. **Evidence before decoration.** Make severity, rule logic, affected entity, source rows, metrics, and recommended action easy to trace.
2. **Calm density.** Support information-rich project-control workflows through hierarchy, alignment, and progressive disclosure rather than oversized cards or excessive whitespace.
3. **Risk is semantic.** Never communicate status with color alone; pair color with labels, icons, and clear language, and reserve strong colors for meaningful states.
4. **Operator momentum.** Optimize the repeated path from workbook upload to validation, findings triage, evidence review, and status update with visible progress and recoverable errors.
5. **Accessible by default.** Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, support keyboard navigation and visible focus, honor reduced-motion preferences, and preserve critical functionality across responsive layouts.
6. **AI through capability.** Express intelligence through deterministic clarity, prioritization, and traceability—not robots, chips, glowing effects, or generic futuristic motifs.
7. **Prove value responsibly.** Instrument meaningful product actions without placing workbook contents or evidence payloads in analytics; make feedback and data deletion easy to find.
